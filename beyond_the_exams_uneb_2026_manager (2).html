Method 1: Netlify Drop (Easiest - 30 Secs)

Visit Netlify Drop

  2. Open app.netlify.com/drop in your web browser.
  3. Drag and drop the folder into the box on the Netlify screen.
  4. Netlify will instantly generate a free live web link (e.g., https://uneb2026.netlify.app) to share with your team!
